## Long version (README and submission text)

This assistant answers factual questions about five HDFC Mutual Fund schemes using only public scheme pages. Every answer is limited to three sentences and cites a single source link so you can check it yourself. It does not recommend buying, selling or holding any fund, does not assess whether a scheme is suitable for you, and does not forecast or compare returns. Figures are reproduced from the source page as captured on the date shown and may since have changed, so confirm anything that matters against the live page or with a registered mutual fund advisor.
