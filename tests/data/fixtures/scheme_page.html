HDFC Large Cap Fund Direct Growth

About the scheme

HDFC Large Cap Fund is an open-ended equity scheme that seeks to provide long-term capital appreciation by investing predominantly in large-cap companies listed in India.

Exit load

An exit load of 1% is charged if units are redeemed within 1 year of investment. No exit load is applicable after 1 year.

How to download your capital gains statement

To download the capital gains statement, log in to the registrar's website and select Reports, then choose the capital gains statement for the relevant financial year and download the PDF.

  • Log in to the registrar website.
  • Open the Reports section.
  • Select the capital gains statement for the financial year.

Benchmark

The scheme is benchmarked against the NIFTY 100 TRI, which is a total return index and therefore assumes reinvestment of dividends.
